Why Pure 2D Deep Learning (e.g. YOLOv8) Fails on Continuous-Track UGVs:

[Limitation] The 2D Bounding Box Trap

A standard 2D object detector detects a "rock", but has zero awareness of physical dimensions. It cannot tell whether the rock is 8 cm (safe for continuous tracks to crawl over) or 28 cm (fatal high-centering risk).

[Limitation] The Pure Depth Blindspot

Raw point clouds and depth maps detect a 20 cm vertical rise, but cannot identify material physics. It treats 20 cm of soft crushable vegetation identically to a 20 cm rigid concrete curb.

[Solution] Hybrid Semantic + 3D Geometric Architecture

Fuses PIDNet-S / MobileNetV4 Semantic Segmentation (material compliance) with Vectorized RANSAC 3D Ground Plane Fitting (exact metric clearance $\Delta h = Z_r - Z_{\text{ground}}$) to execute physics-informed traversability classification in sub-28ms.

OpenMap & OpenStreetMap (OSM) Integration in this UGV Architecture:

1. Global-to-Local Hierarchy

OpenStreetMap (OSM) / OpenMap supplies the global topological route graph (GPS trail waypoints, road networks, satellite elevation priors). Our Stereo Perception Engine acts as the real-time ego-centric metric shield (0-10m) preventing collisions with dynamic off-road hazards.

2. Open-Vocabulary 3D Semantics

With vision-language embeddings (OpenMap/CLIP), natural language queries like "Find a safe dirt trail avoiding high boulders" are projected onto our 2.5D Traversability Costmap to select optimal navigation corridors.

3. Geo-Referenced Costmap Anchoring

Every local costmap cell $(g_x, g_y)$ is transformed via UGV Odometry/GPS into global WGS-84 coordinates: $\begin{bmatrix} \text{Lat} \\ \text{Lon} \end{bmatrix} = T_{\text{base}}^{\text{GPS}} \begin{bmatrix} X_r \\ Y_r \end{bmatrix}$.
